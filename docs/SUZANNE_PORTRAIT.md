# Suzanne — Portrait V1

Créé le 12 octobre 2026 à la demande de Hugo, avec l'outil intégré ImageGen (pas de script ni de clé API du projet). Aucune photographie fournie. Personnage fictif de jeune adulte, environ 18–20 ans. Image générée et inspectée visuellement ; fichier original copié sans modification dans le dépôt.

Asset : `apps/web/public/suzanne-portrait-v1.png`. Utilisé dans la carte d'accueil et la présentation de Suzanne ; le cadrage circulaire de l'avatar est appliqué par CSS. Les textes de l'interface et les alternatives d'image indiquent qu'il s'agit d'une IA fictive. La référence visuelle demandée ne constitue pas une collaboration ou un soutien de la personnalité citée.

## Prompt exact

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: character portrait/avatar for Suzanne, a French lifelong learning app with cream, sage and forest green design.
Create one exquisite realistic editorial portrait of Suzanne, an original fictional young adult woman aged 18 to 20. Visual inspiration: the natural copper-red hair, fair lightly freckled skin, expressive light green eyes and poised warmth associated with a young Jessica Chastain, while giving Suzanne her own distinct facial identity. Long softly curly copper hair with a few natural flyaways, natural skin texture, minimal makeup, intelligent attentive eyes, a small genuine welcoming smile. She should feel like a friendly literature student who loves explaining science, culture and languages.
Scene/backdrop: softly lit warm cream background with very subtle out-of-focus sage tones, clean and quiet.
Composition: square portrait from mid chest up, facing camera, full hair and head inside frame, enough space around the head for a circular avatar crop. A modest cream crew-neck top and relaxed sage cardigan. No props.
Lighting: beautiful soft daylight, contemporary understated editorial photography, realistic rather than glamour retouching, approachable and cultivated.
Constraints: a single person, unmistakably young adult, no text, no logo, no watermark, no collage. This is a fictional AI character, not a real celebrity photograph.
```

```text
Edit the reference portrait of Suzanne. Preserve the identity, adult age around 18–20, copper-red curly hair, pale skin, green eyes, modest cream top and sage cardigan. The user wants a much more realistic result: a believable candid photograph of a real-looking fictional young adult student, not a polished AI avatar or fashion campaign. Natural subtle asymmetry, uneven few freckles, visible natural pores, realistic fine facial hairs, ordinary lightly tired bright eyes, natural lips, imperfect loose hair. A relaxed attentive expression mid-conversation, slight small smile, no glamour posing. Environmental portrait from chest up, looking towards the viewer, taken on a good ordinary camera in soft window light, neutral realistic white balance, subtle natural shadows, no orange filter, no oversoftening, no HDR. Background: a quiet real study room with a barely blurred bookshelf and pale warm wall. Normal depth of field rather than extreme cinematic bokeh. Keep face large enough for an app avatar. Square image, no text, no logos.
```
